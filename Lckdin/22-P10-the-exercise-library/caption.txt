283 exercises are already in the app. 228 of them carry a short demo clip, stored in the app rather than streamed, so they work with no signal.

The gesture worth knowing: hold any exercise name, anywhere in the app, and the movement plays over whatever you were looking at. In your workout, in your history, in the analytics tab. Let go and it disappears.

Fifteen metric presets, because not everything is sets and reps: weight and reps, time under load, distance and pace, assisted, bodyweight plus load, and ten more. The set row shows only the fields that exercise actually needs, so a plank never asks you for a weight.

Missing something? Add it in a minute. Name it, pick how it is measured, tap the muscles it works. Tap a whole group and the share splits between its heads automatically.

And every exercise has its own history: the chart, every session it appeared in, and the records you have set on it.
