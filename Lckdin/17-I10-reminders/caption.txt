Most workout reminders are a daily notification at 6pm that you turn off by Thursday.

Two modes here, so you can set the one that matches how you actually train:

Every N days, for a rolling cycle. Or specific weekdays with a time each: Monday 6pm, Wednesday 7pm, Saturday 9am. Both configurations are stored side by side, so switching between them never throws the other one away.

The reminder tells you what today is according to your split. It does not tell you off for missing yesterday.

One detail worth knowing: the every-N-days mode counts from the calendar, not from the last time you opened the app. Opening the app does not restart the cycle, which is a bug in a surprising number of apps and turns a weekly reminder into a daily one.

And iOS only holds 64 pending notifications before silently dropping the rest, so the app caps its own window and schedules what will really fire.
