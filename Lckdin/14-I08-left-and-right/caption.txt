Single-leg work, dumbbell presses, anything unilateral: your two sides are not the same, and logging them as one number hides exactly the thing you wanted to know.

Split any set from the set menu. Row 1 becomes 1R with 1L underneath. Right first, deliberately, so the row you just tapped does not move under your thumb.

From there the sides are completely independent. Different weight, different reps, different effort. Only the set type carries across the pair, because everything you actually measure is the reason you split it.

Useful for finding a gap you cannot feel (two reps a set is a real difference over a year), and for telling whether rehab is doing anything. When the sides match again, merge them back into one row.
