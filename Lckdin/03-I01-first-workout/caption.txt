Downloaded it and not sure where to start? This is the whole first session.

1. Start empty workout
2. Add an exercise (283 are already in there)
3. Log the set: weight, reps, and reps left in reserve if you want it
4. Let the rest timer run, it keeps going with the phone locked
5. Finish, and any personal records are found for you

No account, no setup, no onboarding to sit through. Save this one and open it at the gym.
