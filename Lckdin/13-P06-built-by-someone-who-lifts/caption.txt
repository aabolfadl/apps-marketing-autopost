You can tell who wrote an app by what it gets right when nobody is looking.

Left and right split into separate rows, with separate values, and nothing ever averages them, because no arithmetic turns 8 reps left and 7 right into one honest number.

Prefilled targets from a plan render grey, as placeholders. Nothing counts until you confirm the set you actually did, so your history never contains a workout that did not happen.

Every session stores the local date it happened on, fixed at the moment you started. Fly across timezones and last Tuesday stays last Tuesday.

Delete a session that held a personal record and the previous holder is restored, by replaying your history. Restore it and the PR comes back.

One set row renders fifteen different kinds of exercise, showing only the fields that one needs, so a plank never asks you for a weight.

None of that is a headline feature. All of it is why the numbers stay true.
