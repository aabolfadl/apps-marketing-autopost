The whole ask is one set.

Not a programme, not a 30-day challenge, not an evening of setup. Open the app, start an empty workout, add an exercise, log one set. Ten seconds.

Here is what that turns into. Week one: your first personal records appear on their own. Week two: the heatmap has enough data to show you a gap you did not know about. Month three: a trend instead of a guess. Year one: a record of every session you did, on your phone, that nobody else has a copy of.

Why it compounds: the ACSM position stand names progressive overload, a steadily increasing demand over time, as a requirement for continued gains. You cannot progress a load you did not write down. That is the argument for logging, in one sentence.
