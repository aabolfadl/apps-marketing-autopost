Resting 60 seconds between heavy sets feels disciplined. It probably costs you.

In a controlled trial, lifters resting 3 minutes between sets gained more strength AND more muscle than lifters resting 1 minute, on an otherwise identical programme (Schoenfeld et al., JSCR, 2016). Same work, different rest, better result.

So let the timer do it. It starts itself when you confirm a set, keeps counting with the phone locked, and sends one notification when you are up. Put the phone down between sets instead of accidentally scrolling for four minutes, or watching the clock for forty seconds.

Three minutes is not a universal law. Heavy compounds want longer, accessories usually want less, and the session has to end eventually. The point is that you picked the number, instead of your phone picking it for you.
