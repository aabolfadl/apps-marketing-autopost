Before you put three years of training into any app, ask what happens if you lose the phone.

Here is the answer. Export everything as JSON (complete and re-importable), CSV (for your own spreadsheet) or the raw SQLite database. Every row of every table, not a summary.

Importing shows you a diff before it writes anything, so you can see exactly what would be added and changed. Then it runs as a single transaction: all of it lands, or none of it does. No half-restored history.

Day to day, deleting a session sends it to Recently Deleted for thirty days. Restore it and your personal records are recalculated, so a record that session held comes back with it.

Takes about ten seconds. Settings, Export, pick a format, send it to yourself. Put the file wherever you like, the app never sees where it goes.
