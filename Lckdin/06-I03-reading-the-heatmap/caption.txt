Every set you log colours a muscle. After two weeks the map stops being a novelty and starts being uncomfortable, usually about rear delts.

How to read it:

Grey is never trained. Cold is trained a little. Hot is trained hard. Grey and cold are deliberately different colours, because "I did two sets" and "I have not touched this in four months" are completely different problems and no serious tool should paint them the same.

Tap any muscle for the actual numbers: hard sets, which exercises fed it, days since you last trained it. Colour is never the only channel.

Three normalisation modes answer three different questions: relative (against your hardest-trained muscle), baseline (against your own history), target (against your weekly set goal).

And the recovery overlay shows what is still recovering rather than what is trained, for mornings when you are not sure legs are a good idea.
