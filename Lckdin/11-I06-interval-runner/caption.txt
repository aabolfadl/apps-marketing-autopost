One of the apps on your phone is an interval timer you open twice a month. You can probably delete it.

Tabata, EMOM, 30/30 and custom ladders run full screen inside Lckdin; as part of the workout you are already logging. Numbers big enough to read from the floor, mid-burpee, with the phone propped against a dumbbell.

It keeps counting with the screen off, so you are not stuck holding an unlocked phone for twelve minutes.

And the rounds you ran get logged like any other work, which means your conditioning actually shows up in your weekly totals instead of disappearing because it happened outside the tracker.
