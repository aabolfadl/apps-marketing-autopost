The hardest part of a gym session is deciding what to do in it. Set a split once and that decision is gone.

Six are built in: Push Pull Legs, Upper Lower, Full Body, Arnold, Bro Split and PHUL. 18 day templates between them, already filled with exercises, set targets and progression rules. Each split shows a weekly muscle preview, so you can see what it actually trains before committing.

The bit most people get wrong: starting a day COPIES the plan into your session. The session then owns its own data. Edit the template months later and your old workouts are untouched. Swap an exercise mid-session and the template is untouched. Both are safe to change.
