If you have ever tried to log a circuit in a gym app, you have probably given up and typed it into a note instead.

Eight kinds of block, all first class: superset, giant set, circuit, complex, pyramid, EMOM, AMRAP and interval. Group the exercises, pick the type, and they get a coloured rail and A1 / A2 / A3 labels.

Adding a round adds a set to every exercise in the block at once, sharing a round number. That is what lets you flip between round-major view while you are training the circuit and exercise-major view when you want to see how one movement progressed.

Reorder whenever you want. The labels rewrite themselves, and sessions you already logged keep the labels they had on the day, so old workouts still read correctly.

Every block type explains itself in the picker. You never have to already know what AMRAP stands for.
