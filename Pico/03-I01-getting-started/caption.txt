New to Pico? Start here. 👋

1️⃣ Home: one card per game. A tick means today’s is done.
2️⃣ Daily or Endless: today’s puzzle, or as many fresh ones as you like.
3️⃣ Stats: streaks, perfect days, best times.
4️⃣ Settings: reminders, haptics, hide the timer.

Bonus: every size and level has its own daily. That’s 18 new puzzles every day.
