What actually happens to your brain on puzzles? 🧠

📚 Exeter and King’s College London (19,000+ adults over 50): people who did word puzzles more often had brain function like someone 10 years younger on grammatical reasoning tests.
📚 Bronx Aging Study: crossword lovers saw fast memory decline begin about 2.5 years later.
📚 Columbia and Duke trial: crosswords beat computerised brain-training games over 78 weeks.

Honest note: much of this shows a link, not proof. But a few minutes of real thinking a day is a habit worth having.
