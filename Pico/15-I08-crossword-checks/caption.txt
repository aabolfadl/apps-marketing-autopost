Stuck on a crossword? Here’s the ladder, from gentle to generous 🪜

1️⃣ Check a square
2️⃣ Check a word
3️⃣ Check the whole puzzle
4️⃣ Hold a square to reveal it (one hint)
5️⃣ Autocheck: every letter checked as you type

Wrong letters get marked, and right ones lock so you stop second-guessing them.

Are you a no-checks purist or an autocheck fan? 👇
