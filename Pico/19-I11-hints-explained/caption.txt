How hints work in Pico 💡

🎁 You start with 10, free
🔁 One balance, shared by all three games
✋ Hold a square, confirm, and it fills in
📺 Out of hints? Only then is a short video offered, for 5 more

And hints never cost you your streak. A hinted solve still counts. It just won’t set a personal best.

Free on the App Store. Link in bio, or search “Pico: Daily Puzzles”.
