Let Pico show you where 👀

1️⃣ Tap any number on the board: Pico shades where it already sits and every row, column and box it can’t go in
2️⃣ Every pencilled copy of that number turns bold
3️⃣ Show mistakes flags errors in red, or switch it off for a purist solve
4️⃣ Limit mistakes ends a run after 1 to 10 wrong entries

Find it all under the sliders button in any sudoku.
