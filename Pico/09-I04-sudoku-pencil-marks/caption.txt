Pencil marks that clean themselves ✏️✨

Place a 4, and every pencilled 4 in that row, column and box disappears on its own. Undo brings them back if you change your mind.

And tap any number on the board: every pencilled copy of it turns bold, so you can see at a glance where it could still go.
