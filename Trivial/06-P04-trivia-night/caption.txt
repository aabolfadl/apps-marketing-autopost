Hosting trivia used to mean an evening of preparation. Finding questions. Checking answers. Printing sheets. Adding up scores while everyone waits.

Then someone argues about question nine.

Combat mode is one phone on a table. Name the teams, set your own point values, start. The questions are already there.

When a team answers, you type what they said and the app rules on it. It handles near misses, missing words and abbreviations, then shows its verdict and the accepted answers. Every verdict is advisory, because scoring is always a manual tap. The room has the final say, as it should.

Free on the App Store. Link in bio.
