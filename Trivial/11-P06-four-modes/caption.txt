Four different games in one free download.

Blitz. Two minutes, solo, chasing a high score. Separate records for every length and category mix.

Strike. No clock at all. Three strikes and the run is over. One difficulty per run so a long run actually means something.

Combat. Teams around a table, one phone passed between them. You set the scoring rules, the app judges the answers, the room overrules it.

Quiz Board. You write the categories, the clues, the answers and the point values. We supply nothing but the board.

You only need one of them to sound good. The rest come along anyway.
