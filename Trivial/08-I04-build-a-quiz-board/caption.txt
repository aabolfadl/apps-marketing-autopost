Quiz Board is the mode where we supply nothing.

No questions from our set. You write the category names, the clues, the answers and the point values. All of it.

1. Start a board and name it after the occasion.
2. Each column is a category. Name it whatever you want.
3. Tap a cell, write the question, the answer and what it is worth.
4. Host it. Tap a cell, read it out, award the points.

The best boards are the personal ones. How well do you know me, for a birthday. Office lore, for a Friday. The year you were born, for a milestone.

Boards are saved, so you can reuse them. Free on the App Store, link in bio.
