Six things Trivial does that most people never find.

1. It saves your party game. Leave a Combat night or a Quiz Board halfway and the Home screen offers to resume it, scores and turn order intact, even after a restart.

2. The flag button is nuclear. Flagging one question retires its entire topic, in every mode, permanently. Use it on the subject you always groan at.

3. Four correct in a row starts a multiplier. It builds up to double points, and one wrong answer resets it.

4. You can name a category mix and reuse it. One tap in every mode afterwards.

5. Keep screen awake lives in Settings. Turn it on before a long Combat night.

6. It remembers what you have seen. Recent questions move to the back of the deck, so back to back rounds stay fresh without ever running out.
