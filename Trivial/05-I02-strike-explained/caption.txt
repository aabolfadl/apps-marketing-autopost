Strike is Blitz with the clock taken out and the stakes put in.

No timer anywhere. You can sit on a question for a minute if you nearly have it. But every wrong answer costs a strike, and when they are gone the run is over.

Set it to three strikes, or one if you want it to hurt. One difficulty level per run, so a long run actually means something.

Two records are kept: your best score and your longest run. The second one is the one people chase.

Free on the App Store, link in bio.
