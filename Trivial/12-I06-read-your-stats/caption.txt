Trivial does not give you one score. It gives you a map.

Your best is tracked for every combination of length, difficulty and categories, so three minutes of Expert Science never competes with one minute of mixed Easy. No setup flatters you.

Every answer also credits every level of its category path. So you can open Geography, drill into Flags, and find the exact corner that keeps catching you out.

Tracked: best round, longest Strike run, accuracy per topic at any depth, and your best streak.

The category breakdown counts Blitz and Strike together, because what you know about a subject does not depend on which mode asked.

All of it clears from Settings whenever you want. None of it was ever sent anywhere.
