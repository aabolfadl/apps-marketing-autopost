The complete list of what Trivial collects about you:

Nothing.

No account system. No analytics. No crash reporting. No server of its own. It does not ask for your contacts, your location, your photos or your microphone, because it has no use for any of them.

Apple's own privacy label on the listing reads Data Not Collected. That is their assessment, not our marketing.

Your scores, your saved boards and your settings live in your phone's storage and nowhere else. We cannot see them. Delete the app and they are genuinely gone.

You will also never see an "allow tracking" prompt, because every ad request is flagged non personalised. There is nothing to ask permission for.
