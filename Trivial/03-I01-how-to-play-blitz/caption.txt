How to play Blitz, the two minute solo mode.

1. Pick a round length. One, two or three minutes.
2. Pick your difficulty levels. Five to choose from, and you can mix them.
3. Pick your categories. A whole subject, or one narrow slice of it.
4. Play. Four options per question, harder questions pay more.

Four right answers in a row starts a multiplier that builds up to double points. Protect the streak.

Afterwards you get a breakdown by category, which is usually humbling.
