3️⃣ Offline mode: switch off exchange-rate updates and Cents works entirely offline
4️⃣ Backups: there’s no cloud copy by design, so keep your backup file somewhere safe
5️⃣ Erase all data: type DELETE and it’s gone, with no copy anywhere else
