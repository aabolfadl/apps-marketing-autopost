Did you know you can tap the BACK of your iPhone to run a shortcut? 🤯

With Cents, two taps = a logged expense:
👆👆 double-tap the back of your phone
💬 “How much?” → type the amount
💬 “What for?” → type what it was
✅ recorded and categorised, and Cents never even opens

Setup (once, ~2 minutes):
1️⃣ Cents › Settings › Fast logging
2️⃣ Build the shortcut (Cents shows every step as a screen recording)
3️⃣ Settings › Accessibility › Touch › Back Tap › Double Tap → your shortcut
