Your money is nobody’s business. Not even ours. 🔒

Cents has:
❌ no account
❌ no server holding your data
❌ no analytics, trackers or ads
❌ no access to your bank login

Everything lives on your iPhone. The only thing the app ever sends is a currency code, to fetch exchange rates, and you can switch even that off.

Don’t take our word for it: Apple’s privacy label on the App Store says “Data Not Collected”. Check it before you download.

Free on the App Store. Link in bio, or search “Cents: Finance Tracker”.
