Wrong category? Fix it from the Lock Screen 🔓

When Cents logs a bank SMS, you get a notification. Long-press it and you’ll see:
🗑️ Discard
🏷️ three category buttons

Choose which three in Settings › Notification buttons (or let Cents pick your most-used).

Change a vendor’s category once and Cents asks: “Always use Groceries for CARREFOUR?” Say yes and it remembers.
Free on the App Store. Link in bio, or search “Cents: Finance Tracker”.
